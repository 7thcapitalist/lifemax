# Vision

## Why this exists

LifeMax is a character operating system: it turns real-life progress into an
RPG-like feedback loop without reducing a person to a single score. The
promise is "do not merely track your life — play it." The user defines the
kind of person they want to become; the product translates that identity into
domains, quests, evidence of progress, reflections, and course corrections.

## The bet (1–2 years)

That a multidimensional character sheet plus quests plus balance feedback can
make personal progress feel motivating rather than guilt-driven, in a way a
conventional productivity tracker or todo app does not. The MVP question is
whether someone would voluntarily open this every morning and every Sunday
because it helps them live better — not because they feel guilty.

## Who this is for

Right now: the founder, as a single-owner personal system. This is
deliberately not built for "everyone" yet — see USERS.md.

## Non-goals

- A social network or public leaderboard.
- One universal life score that collapses a person into a single number.
- Connecting every external service immediately — integrations are earned,
  not assumed.
- Medical, financial, or mental-health diagnosis.
- AI or telemetry enabled by default — both are explicitly opt-in
  (`AI_MODE`/telemetry flags default to disabled).
