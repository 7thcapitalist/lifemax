# Competitive context

## What users do today instead

Conventional productivity trackers and todo apps, or spreadsheets, or
nothing at all. These tend to collapse a person into output metrics
(tasks done, streaks) and don't represent a whole life.

## Our wedge

A multidimensional character sheet plus a quest framing that rewards living
(relationships, recovery, adventure, joy are first-class stats, not
afterthoughts), with automated evidence but human-decided meaning — the
product observes, the user still decides what matters. Privacy is part of
the product, not a bolt-on: no external AI or telemetry unless explicitly
enabled.

## Not competing on

- Breadth of integrations (deliberately earned slowly, not day one).
- Being a multi-user product or company — this is personal software for now;
  see ROADMAP.md for when/whether that's reconsidered.
- Social features, leaderboards, or gamified competition with other people.
